How these are counted
In range
3.9 to 10.0 mmol/L. The goals are the international targets for CGM.
A low
At least 15 minutes below 3.9. Very low = below 3.0.
Typical curve
The middle value for each half hour. The darker band holds half of the readings, the lighter band 9 in 10.
Est. HbA1c
GMI, calculated from the average. It often differs from a lab value.

Settings

Appearance

Dark is easier on the eyes at night. System follows your computer or phone.

Your insulin

The mealtime insulin is shown on each meal you log. Both are given to the local AI, so its answers know what you take.

History

Fills in older readings from a LibreView CSV export. Safe to run more than once.

Startup

It starts minimized in the taskbar, so Telegram messages are read as soon as the computer is on. Close that window to stop it. The local AI (Ollama) is started too when needed.

Telegram

Sundays at 19:00 and on the 1st of each month at 09:00: time in range, lows and average, never individual readings. It goes through Telegram. You can also ask for one any time with /week or /month.
