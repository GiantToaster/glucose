## What's working
1. Zone-trace curve as signature; glucose meaning carried by colour and text consistently everywhere.
2. Dose check is a typo guard, not advice (own usual dose, extra press, ochre, moved out of dose choice).
3. Careful stale/a11y handling (full-colour old value + age tag, rich aria-label, WAI-ARIA combobox/tabs, 44px touch).
