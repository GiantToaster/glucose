## Design specificity verdict
Authored in the data, generic in the shell. Meal→outcome loop ("6.9 → 12.3 peak (+5.4)"), in-range-start comparison, lows-per-half-hour strip and Swedish free text are product-specific. The chrome (gradient-square logo, "Glucose" wordmark, indigo accent, identical rounded cards) is interchangeable with any SaaS panel. The glucose trace is the only element with no colour meaning.

## What's working
1. Hero is a true at-a-glance surface (value, trend, age, last meal, 24 h in range; value in tab title).
2. Meal→outcome loop is the product's real idea and is honest (comparison only counts meals started in range).
3. Trustworthy plain-language copy and forgiving interactions (undo, usual-dose fill, clear errors).

## Priority issues
- [P1] Dose dialog compares other doses at the moment of dosing (mealWarn "Other doses…" in ⚠ box next to the dose stepper). Fix: show only history for the entered dose on one line; move "Other doses" to the meal's detail view; neutral styling unless a past low is involved. Command: clarify, then quieter.
- [P1] High/low states encoded only by colour; stale value fades to 45 %; chart trace monochrome; no state text for screen readers. Fix: state word (Low/High/In range) + tinted hero edge when low; full-contrast stale value with "Last reading N min ago"; zone-coloured trace; label 3.9 on the axis. Command: colorize + harden.
- [P2] Phone activity list: 48 px time column wraps "Yesterday"; 44 px bordered ✕ on every row dominates the thumb zone; placeholder truncated. Fix: wider column / "Yest."; on touch open detail on row tap and move Remove there; borderless muted ✕ on desktop. Command: adapt.
- [P2] Doses not checked against own history (75 vs 7.5, 40 vs usual 4) in dialog and free text. Fix: non-blocking "40 units is 10× your usual 4 for X. Log anyway?" and dose prominent in the Undo toast. Command: harden.
- [P2] Two answers to "when do I go low?" (Insights vs Patterns use different day-part buckets and periods), "U" vs "units", flat heading scale. Fix: shared day-part vocabulary, period in each heading, "units" everywhere, clearer heading steps. Command: clarify + typeset.
